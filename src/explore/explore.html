Enjoy your exploration!

Demographics

We display below the different categories used for the World Values Survey. Please note that we regrouped some categories to make the chart more readable, and that some categories were not available for all countries.

Select between Age, Sex, Religion, Education, Economic Class, and Ethnicity. Hover over each slice to see exact percentages.

We notice that while the dataset shows relatively balanced distributions for categories like sex and age groups, some categories like ethnicity show high diversity. Many ethnic groups were consolidated into an "Others" category due to their smaller representation in the dataset. This consolidation helps maintain readability while acknowledging the rich diversity of the survey respondents.

Similarities between individuals

Finally, we wanted to know if any clusters of individuals exist, and if so, what characteristics they share. We thus present the chart below, where each bubble represents an individual out of 30% of the dataset. Hovering over the bubbles, you can see the demographic characteristics of the individuals, and decide for yourself what explains the clusters!

Choose to highlight individuals by:

As you can see above, no clear clusters of individuals exist, and the individuals are spread out across the chart.

This is an important message to keep in mind: people are more than their demographic labels. These charts are a starting point, but every individual brings unique experiences and perspectives that can’t be captured by age, gender, education or background alone.
